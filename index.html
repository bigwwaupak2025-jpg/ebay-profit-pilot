eBay Profit Pilot

Chrome extension for eBay.com.au sellers sourcing from AliExpress. Official eBay AU fees, AUD, GST included.

What it does

1 · SupplierReads the price, shipping (free or paid), discount and your total cost from the open AliExpress page.
2 · eBay AUFinds the same product on eBay Australia, with sold and active prices: lowest, median, highest.
3 · Your priceTakes off eBay's fee and postage and recommends the price that keeps your minimum profit ($8).
4 · SEOOptional: writes an AI title, keywords, item specifics and a description.
i · ReferenceOfficial eBay fees by price, plus postage within Australia and overseas.

Install (2 minutes)

⬇ Download extension (.zip)

  1. Unzip the downloaded file into a folder, for example ebay-profit-pilot.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Click the puzzle icon 🧩 and pin eBay Profit Pilot.
  5. Open an AliExpress product page, click the extension icon, and press ▶ Run all steps.

Stay logged in to eBay.com.au in Chrome; it helps the competitor search. For AI SEO, add your Anthropic API key in step 4. The key is stored only in your browser.

Before you list, check

  • Set AliExpress to Ship to: Australia and AUD, so the price and shipping match what you will pay.
  • In step 2, untick eBay listings that are not the same product.
  • Choose the right parcel size; postage is the biggest cost after the product.
